# Headlines Examples

Schwartz examples demonstrating headline verbalization and finishing principles. Examples are lightly cleaned or paraphrased to avoid OCR artifacts while preserving the original product, structure, and terminology.

### Example 1: Viceroy - measuring the size of the claim

> "20,000 filter traps in Viceroy" turns a filter benefit into a counted, concrete mass of protection.

**Principle illustrated:** A numerical measure makes the claim feel larger and more definite.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles

### Example 2: Bayer Aspirin - measuring the speed of the claim

> Bayer's headline says that in two seconds the aspirin begins dissolving in the glass.

**Principle illustrated:** Speed can be measured before the final benefit is experienced, making relief feel immediate.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-concepts

### Example 3: Rolls Royce - demonstrating by a prime example

> At 60 miles an hour, the loudest noise in the Rolls Royce is the electric clock.

**Principle illustrated:** One vivid proof-scene demonstrates the whole claim better than abstract superiority.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, video-scripts

### Example 4: Piano ad - dramatizing the result

> "They laughed when I sat down at the piano - but when I started to play..." turns the claim into a social scene.

**Principle illustrated:** The result becomes more desirable when dramatized as reversal, status, and surprise.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, video-scripts, ad-concepts

### Example 5: Bald-headed barber - stating the claim as paradox

> A bald-headed barber is presented as the man who saved the writer's hair.

**Principle illustrated:** A contradiction makes the reader stop long enough to resolve it.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles

### Example 6: Camels and Mickey Mantle - identification

> Mickey Mantle says Camels never bother his throat.

**Principle illustrated:** The claim borrows authority and identity from a person the prospect recognizes or admires.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-concepts

### Example 7: Whiter wash - question form

> "Who else wants a whiter wash - with no hard work?" asks the prospect to self-select into the desire.

**Principle illustrated:** A question turns a claim into the reader's own mental answer.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles, email-copy

### Example 8: How to Win Friends and Influence People - information promise

> The title promises information about how to win friends and influence people.

**Principle illustrated:** A how-to headline sells the method as much as the result.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, funnel-copy, email-copy

### Example 9: Boss mechanic - authority tied to the claim

> A boss mechanic shows how to avoid engine repair bills.

**Principle illustrated:** The authority source makes the claim feel practical and earned.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-concepts

### Example 10: Coldene - before-and-after

> Before Coldene, the child endures days of aching, sneezing, wheezing, dripping, suffering, coughing, crying, gagging, and spitting; with Coldene, the ad compresses the recovery promise into the contrast.

**Principle illustrated:** Before-and-after structure intensifies pain before presenting relief.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, video-scripts, ad-concepts

### Example 11: Guided Missile Spark Plugs - newness

> "Announcing! Guided Missile Spark Plugs!" makes the ordinary product category feel like a new technological event.

**Principle illustrated:** Newness can refresh a claim before the mechanism is fully explained.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles

### Example 12: Toni twins - challenge

> "Which twin has the Toni? And which has the $15 permanent?" turns the claim into a test the reader wants to solve.

**Principle illustrated:** A challenge creates participation and proof at the same time.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, video-scripts

### Example 13: Ring and piston job in a tube - condensation

> "Now! A ring and piston job in a tube!" makes the product stand in for the repair it replaces.

**Principle illustrated:** Condensing product and replacement creates a compact reason to care.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-concepts

### Example 14: Atlantic Ocean - symbolizing the claim

> "Starting next Tuesday, the Atlantic Ocean becomes only one-fifth as long" symbolizes faster travel by shrinking the ocean.

**Principle illustrated:** A parallel reality can make an abstract improvement instantly visual.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, video-scripts, ad-concepts

### Example 15: Tommy Armour - contradicting the expected mechanism

> Tommy Armour tells the golfer to hit hard with the right hand, contradicting what the reader expects about the swing.

**Principle illustrated:** A mechanism that violates belief creates curiosity and opens the body copy.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles

### Example 16: Alka-Seltzer - naming the problem

> "When you're weary with daytime fatigue, take Alka-Seltzer" gives the condition a recognizable name before offering the product.

**Principle illustrated:** Naming the need helps the prospect identify his discomfort as a solvable problem.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles, email-copy

### Example 17: Vacation ad - addressing people who cannot buy

> "If you've already taken your vacation, don't read this. It'll break your heart."

**Principle illustrated:** Excluding the wrong reader can intensify desire in everyone still qualified.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, email-copy

### Example 18: Candleholder - dramatizing production difficulty

> When Jens finished designing the candleholder, the maker had to invent a whole new kind of candle.

**Principle illustrated:** Difficulty of production becomes proof that the claim is unusual.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-concepts

### Example 19: Avis - reinforcement

> "We try harder" becomes stronger when joined to the reason: "We're only second."

**Principle illustrated:** Two images can combine into a new identity more powerful than either line alone.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-concepts

### Example 20: Flowers without dirtying your hands - momentum

> "How to Grow Thousands of Flowers - Without Dirtying Your Hands" opens a benefit and a contradiction the reader must resolve.

**Principle illustrated:** A headline can create momentum by raising a question the body copy completes.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles, video-scripts

### Example 21: Volkswagen - mood through restraint

> Schwartz points to Volkswagen copy as using absence of expected adjectives, whimsy, and self-kidding to create sincerity and trust.

**Principle illustrated:** Tone can be built by what the headline or copy refuses to exaggerate.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-concepts
