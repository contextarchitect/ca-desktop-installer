# Intensification Examples

Schwartz examples demonstrating Intensification principles. Examples are paraphrased with light cleanup for OCR artifacts while preserving the original product, scenario, and structural beats.

### Example 1: Nearly Wild rose bush - direct detailed claim

> Schwartz's rose ad opens with agricultural-college bloom counts, then makes the reader picture one bush giving thousands of roses, hand-sized clusters, rooms filled with fragrance, and a yard turned into the neighborhood showplace.

**Principle illustrated:** First present the product or satisfaction directly, bluntly, and in complete sensory detail.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, ad-concepts, headlines-universal

### Example 2: Christmas flashlight projector - result made visible

> The projector ad has the family switch on the device and see Bible scenes life-sized on the living-room wall: the Nativity, the Star of Bethlehem, the Sermon on the Mount, and miracles becoming immediate for children.

**Principle illustrated:** Give desire a concrete scene of fulfillment so the prospect can experience the result before purchase.

**Relevance tag:** video-scripts, funnel-copy, ad-concepts

### Example 3: Pan American plastic model jet - physical appearance

> The model-airplane ad dwells on the table-top reveal: triple landing gear, six-inch tail section, silver wings and engines, white fuselage, blue stripe, Pan American globe, American flag, swept wings, and jet sound simulator.

**Principle illustrated:** Intensify by making the product's form and detail tangible before describing performance.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, video-scripts

### Example 4: Plastic model jet - product in action

> After the visual reveal, Schwartz's model-jet copy has the child attach the U-Control line, swing the plane into flight, send it into wider circles, soar, stall, dive, recover, land, race, and fly in formation.

**Principle illustrated:** Put the claims in action; show not only what the product is, but exactly how it produces the thrill.

**Relevance tag:** video-scripts, ad-concepts, funnel-copy

### Example 5: Self-propelled fish lure - mechanism dramatized

> The lure is fueled, cast into water, floats quietly, shudders, springs to life, buzzes like a dying bee, dives like a maimed minnow, rises again, and keeps roaming even when the fisherman is idle or asleep.

**Principle illustrated:** A product-in-action scene can make mechanism, benefit, and emotional payoff arrive together.

**Relevance tag:** video-scripts, ad-angles, ad-concepts

### Example 6: Car power booster - reader inside first ownership day

> The reader goes to the same tired car next weekend, turns the ignition, feels the engine come alive, pulls beside a similar car at a light, lets the other driver start first, then surges ahead and sees astonishment in the rear-view mirror.

**Principle illustrated:** Bring in the reader by scripting the first day of ownership and the social proof moment.

**Relevance tag:** video-scripts, ad-angles, funnel-copy

### Example 7: SA Fire Injectors - reader-performed test

> The spark-plug ad tells automatic-transmission owners, racing-car owners, boat owners, and standard-shift drivers what to measure before installation, then what increased idle movement or RPM should prove afterward.

**Principle illustrated:** Turn demonstration into a test the reader imagines performing for himself.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, ad-concepts, video-scripts

### Example 8: Push-Button Memory book - one-evening proof

> The memory-course ad sends the reader to page 39, has him read eight pages, memorize twenty facts, put the list away, then amaze friends, family, customers, or household members the next morning.

**Principle illustrated:** Make proof immediate by promising a specific short test and a recognizable next-day result.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, email-copy, ad-angles

### Example 9: English plant food pellets - benefits stretched in time

> The plant-food ad unfolds in stages: first a burst of growth, then strengthened cells and sturdier stems, then a third ingredient reaches the buds and the garden erupts in color while neighbors come to inspect the flowers.

**Principle illustrated:** Stretch benefits in time so one claim becomes a sequence of fresh future satisfactions.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, email-copy, video-scripts

### Example 10: Reducing plan case histories - audience as proof scene

> Schwartz's reducing ad uses doctor-supervised men and women who eat familiar foods, report more pep, lose inches and pounds, bring old clothes out of storage, and recover a younger look.

**Principle illustrated:** Bring in an audience through case history so other people's outcomes renew the product image.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, funnel-copy, email-copy

### Example 11: Nearly Wild rose experts - expert astonishment

> The rose ad returns to the college test field and contrasts famous roses with Nearly Wild's bloom count, then shows rose experts reacting with disbelief at one bush performing like a complete rose garden.

**Principle illustrated:** Expert approval is strongest when experts are surprised by a result in their own field.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, funnel-copy, headlines-universal

### Example 12: Dollar Book Club - side-by-side savings contrast

> The radio commercial repeats the same contrast three times: friends pay full retail prices for current best sellers, while Dollar Book Club members receive the same books for 99 cents.

**Principle illustrated:** Compare, contrast, and prove superiority by placing old buying behavior and new advantage in parallel.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, email-copy, headlines-universal

### Example 13: Fuel Reclaimer - old waste versus new value

> The Fuel Reclaimer copy turns escaping crankcase smoke into a visible enemy, then reverses it into extra fuel feeding the engine, replacing polluted oil, repair bills, and sludge with young-car power and economy.

**Principle illustrated:** Contrast makes technical superiority emotionally clear when the old condition is made concrete.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, funnel-copy, video-scripts

### Example 14: Power Flash spark plug - black side before solution

> The spark-plug ad asks the reader to inspect an old plug, see carbon choking the firing point, then shifts to the new plug with hundreds of potential firing points that burns away carbon and keeps firing clean.

**Principle illustrated:** Picture the black side first, then apply the product as the salve that removes the wound.

**Relevance tag:** video-scripts, funnel-copy, ad-angles

### Example 15: Oil additive - ease magnifies benefit

> The car-additive passage has the reader lift the hood and pour one can into the oil filler pipe; thirty seconds of work then becomes better mileage, longer oil life, engine protection, and delayed repair expense.

**Principle illustrated:** Show how easy it is to get the benefits by contrasting tiny effort with large payoff.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, ad-angles, email-copy

### Example 16: Sherwin Cody English Course - metaphor and personification

> The English-course ad imagines Mr. Cody standing forever at the student's elbow, quietly correcting every mispronunciation, spelling error, wrong word, and grammatical mistake until correct usage becomes habitual.

**Principle illustrated:** Use metaphor, analogy, and imagination to dramatize an instructional mechanism.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, video-scripts, funnel-copy

### Example 17: Blow-torch kit - application catalog

> The blow-torch kit ad compresses many homeowner uses into a rapid list: paint removal, soldering, thawing pipes and locks, starting fires, burning weeds, loosening bolts, car-body repairs, jewelry work, and hobbies.

**Principle illustrated:** A catalog expands desire horizontally by showing many possible applications near the close.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, email-copy, ad-angles

### Example 18: Memory book catalog - desire piled upon desire

> The memory-book ad lists names and faces, speeches, sales presentations, cards, vocabulary, foreign languages, jokes, appointments, addresses, and telephone numbers as one mounting set of mental powers.

**Principle illustrated:** A catalog can deepen desire vertically by piling one desirable outcome on another.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, email-copy, ad-concepts

### Example 19: Fish lure summary - closing magnitude stack

> The fish-lure close stacks freed labor, saved bait money, no ordinary dead lure, fishing while asleep, big hauls, and the pleasure of watching other fishermen react to the self-propelled lure in action.

**Principle illustrated:** Before closing, summarize the product's performances so magnitude itself supplies a final push.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, email-copy, ad-angles

### Example 20: Power Flash spark plug guarantee - guarantee as summary

> The spark-plug guarantee asks the buyer to test for power, driving performance, and gas savings, then ties each promised gain to money back or replacement over a two-year guarantee.

**Principle illustrated:** Put the guarantee to work by making risk reversal repeat the core performances.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, email-copy

### Example 21: Magic English Pellets guarantee - guaranteed use cases

> The plant-food guarantee tells the reader to create super-flowers, super-vegetables, use the pellets across the garden and on hard-luck plants, then return the empty package if the garden does not become a showplace.

**Principle illustrated:** A guarantee can intensify by naming the exact results and applications it protects.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, email-copy, ad-concepts

### Example 22: Colgate Gardol - central campaign idea

> Schwartz cites the Colgate campaign built around Gardol as an invisible shield protecting teeth, with changing opening demonstrations before the repeated shield-to-teeth explanation.

**Principle illustrated:** Campaign intensification compresses the dominant image and renews it through varied demonstrations.

**Relevance tag:** video-scripts, ad-concepts, headlines-universal

### Example 23: Toni and Clairol - Rule and Example campaign

> Toni repeats the twin question while changing the girls pictured above it; Clairol repeats the natural-hair-color question while changing women and settings, so each illustration makes the same verbal rule new again.

**Principle illustrated:** The "Rule and Example" pattern preserves the verbal image while fresh illustrations renew attention.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, headlines-universal

### Example 24: Volkswagen magazine campaign - building-block intensification

> Schwartz describes Volkswagen ads such as "Think Small," "Lemon," and "Our new model" as short, sharply focused sales stories around the same product, accumulating utility, economy, durability, service, and honesty.

**Principle illustrated:** A campaign can intensify by keeping the product as the fixed focal point while each ad reveals a new facet.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-concepts, ad-angles
