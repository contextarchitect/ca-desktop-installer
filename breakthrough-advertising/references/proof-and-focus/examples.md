# Proof + Focus Examples

Schwartz examples showing Redefinition, Mechanization, Concentration, and Camouflage in action.

### Example 1: Lifebuoy soap - odor becomes proof

> Lifebuoy's medicinal odor could not be removed without weakening the product, so the campaign made body odor the enemy and the soap's strong smell proof of odor-destroying power.

**Principle illustrated:** Redefinition can turn a liability into an asset when the liability becomes evidence of the promised result.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, ad-concepts

### Example 2: TV Repair Manual - repairs become outside adjustments

> The ad avoids "repair" until the reader accepts that most problems are warning signals or symptoms corrected by minor outside adjustments.

**Principle illustrated:** Simplification replaces a frightening task with a familiar, manageable action.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, video-scripts

### Example 3: Sherwin Cody English course - English becomes a few recurring patterns

> The ad reduces English to sixty-nine common words, a dozen punctuation principles, and twenty-five typical grammar errors requiring only fifteen minutes a day.

**Principle illustrated:** Redefinition makes a hard subject easy by breaking it into a small number of mastered parts.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, email-copy

### Example 4: Word Power Machine - English becomes persuasion power

> A later English-course ad shifts from avoiding grammar mistakes to gaining a vocabulary that makes speech and writing more persuasive and exciting.

**Principle illustrated:** Escalation broadens a narrow benefit into a larger, more desired life outcome.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, headlines-universal, funnel-copy

### Example 5: Spark plugs - 99-cent part against a $2,000 car

> The copy makes a small part feel important by showing it can rob a costly car of its real power and enjoyment.

**Principle illustrated:** Escalation ties a low-cost product to a high-value possession and a larger emotional payoff.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, headlines-universal

### Example 6: SA Fire Injectors - price compared to handmade value

> The ad says the injectors would have to sell for far more if practically made by hand, then presents $1.49 each as only a fraction of their value.

**Principle illustrated:** Price reduction changes the comparison frame so a premium price feels like a bargain.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, email-copy

### Example 7: Rinso - dirt floats off in suds

> After promising a whiter wash without hard work, Rinso explains that soaking in rich suds lets dirt and stains float off before a gentle rinse.

**Principle illustrated:** Mechanization answers "How?" at the point where desire would otherwise become skepticism.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, funnel-copy

### Example 8: Xenophon camera - mechanism named only

> The camera ad supports foolproof photos by naming familiar mechanisms such as an electronic light setter, push-button lens, and magazine load.

**Principle illustrated:** When the market already understands the mechanism, naming it may be enough.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, headlines-universal

### Example 9: Mechanism headlines - new way as main claim

> Headlines such as "Run your car without spark plugs" and "Shrinks hemorrhoids without surgery" put the mechanism or contrast directly on top of the ad.

**Principle illustrated:** In sophisticated markets, the mechanism can become the headline because it offers a new way to satisfy the desire.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles

### Example 10: Robert Collier Madras shirts - bargain mechanism

> Collier explains that an old reliable mill was overstocked because seasonal demand failed, so the advertiser bought a quarter-million yards at unusual savings.

**Principle illustrated:** A bargain gains belief when the copy explains why the seller can offer it.

**Relevance tag:** email-copy, funnel-copy

### Example 11: Fire injectors versus spark plugs - bad-good contrast

> The copy alternates spark plug weaknesses with fire injector strengths: air gap versus surface conductor, misfiring versus full explosion, cleaning versus no servicing.

**Principle illustrated:** Concentration uses repeated one-for-one contrast to weaken the old solution and prove the new one.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, ad-concepts

### Example 12: Reducing pill - passive plans versus active fat burning

> The ad defines ordinary reducing plans as passive hunger-appeasing products, dramatizes the repeated failure of will power, then introduces the active pill and plan.

**Principle illustrated:** Concentration can first sharpen the familiar failure of current methods, then shift into mechanism, documentation, and promise.

**Relevance tag:** funnel-copy, video-scripts

### Example 13: Wall Street Journal adaptation - format camouflage

> A handling-people ad became far more successful after adopting Journal type, subhead treatment, bars, column conventions, and line drawing style.

**Principle illustrated:** Matching the trusted medium's format transfers part of its believability to the ad.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, funnel-copy

### Example 14: Skin specialist blackhead ad - news phraseology

> The ad opens with a specialist demonstrating a treatment, a byline, and a New York dateline, giving the copy the atmosphere of a report.

**Principle illustrated:** News indicators can make the ad feel like information rather than a sales story.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, headlines-universal

### Example 15: Volkswagen - understatement

> The Volkswagen ad calmly notes that there is no radiator, hoses, water pump, rust, anti-freeze worry, or cracked block, then stops.

**Principle illustrated:** Understatement can create sincerity by avoiding the expected advertising surplus of adjectives and claims.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, video-scripts, email-copy

### Example 16: Presidency handbook - deadly sincerity

> The ad says it is for the man who will settle for nothing less than the presidency of his firm and is willing to make the sacrifices necessary to get there.

**Principle illustrated:** Deadly sincerity gains belief by excluding most readers and frankly stating the demanded sacrifice.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, email-copy, funnel-copy
