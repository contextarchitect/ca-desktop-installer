# Sophistication Stages Examples

Schwartz examples showing how markets evolve as competing ads exhaust claims and mechanisms.

### Example 1: Reducing field - First Stage

> In a newly opened reducing market, the winning headline could simply state the mass desire: "NOW! LOSE UGLY FAT!" The market had not yet been saturated with similar promises, so no elaborate mechanism or indirection was needed.

**Principle illustrated:** In the first stage, the direct need or claim can carry the headline because the promise is still new.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles

### Example 2: Reducing field - Second Stage

> After the reducing market had already heard the direct claim, successful ads enlarged it: "LOSE UP TO 47 POUNDS IN 4 WEEKS - OR RECEIVE $40 BACK!" and "I AM 61 POUNDS LIGHTER ... NEVER A HUNGRY MINUTE."

**Principle illustrated:** In the second stage, the advertiser outbids the existing claim by making the result larger, faster, and more concrete.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-concepts

### Example 3: Home garden field - Claim enlargement

> A Floribunda Rose ad promised "PICK 25 - 50 - 100 ROSES FROM THIS ONE MAGNIFICENT PLANT!" Later, a cushion mum promised "SIX HUNDRED MUMS FROM A SINGLE BUSH!" Then another headline pushed the limit: "WHO EVER HEARD OF 17,000 BLOOMS FROM A SINGLE PLANT?"

**Principle illustrated:** Second-stage enlargement often escalates in measurable numbers until the promise approaches the edge of belief.

**Relevance tag:** headlines-universal, ad-angles

### Example 4: Reducing field - Third Stage mechanism

> Once weight-loss prospects had heard dozens of extreme claims, new ads shifted from the amount lost to the way it happened: "FLOATS FAT RIGHT OUT OF YOUR BODY!" and "FIRST WONDER DRUG FOR REDUCING!" The old claim was then restated in the lead with proof and benefit detail.

**Principle illustrated:** In the third stage, a new mechanism restores freshness and believability to an old promise.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, funnel-copy, headlines-universal

### Example 5: Reducing field - Fourth Stage mechanism enlargement

> After mechanism-led reducing ads began to work, the mechanism itself became the object of enlargement: "FIRST NO-DIET REDUCING WONDER DRUG!" The promise was not merely weight loss, but a more desirable version of the mechanism.

**Principle illustrated:** In the fourth stage, the advertiser elaborates the mechanism by making it easier, more attractive, or more complete.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, ad-concepts, headlines-universal

### Example 6: Postum - Fifth Stage identification

> Schwartz points back to the Postum headline "WHY MEN CRACK ..." as an example of reviving a product whose market no longer wanted to hear product claims. The ad reached prospects through identification before asking them to consider the product again.

**Principle illustrated:** In the fifth stage, the copy must bring the prospect into the ad through self-recognition rather than through another claim.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, video-scripts, email-copy

### Example 7: Cigarette industry - Full five-stage cycle

> Cigarette advertising began with direct pleasure claims such as "I'D WALK A MILE FOR A CAMEL!" and "CHESTERFIELD - THEY SATISFY!" It then broadened claims, introduced mechanisms such as "LUCKIES - THEY'RE TOASTED!" and "CAMELS - PROTECT YOUR T-ZONE!", elaborated mechanisms with health and format claims, and finally moved into identification through Marlboro-style virile men imagery.

**Principle illustrated:** A large market can pass through all five stages as each successful approach is copied, enlarged, and exhausted.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, video-scripts, funnel-copy

### Example 8: Women smokers - Fifth Stage taboo market

> To open the women's smoking market after the First World War, the ad could not directly tell women to smoke or even show a woman smoking. Instead, it showed a young couple on a moonlit beach, with the woman's headline: "BLOW SOME MY WAY."

**Principle illustrated:** In an identification problem, a four-word situation can carry the invitation that a direct claim could not safely make.

**Relevance tag:** ad-concepts, video-scripts, headlines-universal

### Example 9: Filter cigarettes - Submarket sophistication cycle

> The cancer scare opened a mass market for filter cigarettes, and that submarket replayed the cycle: "KENT'S MICRONITE FILTER TRAPS TARS BEFORE THEY REACH YOUR LIPS"; "20,000 FILTER TRAPS IN VICEROY!"; "PARLIAMENT - THE MOST IMPORTANT 1/4 INCH IN SMOKING TODAY - NO FILTER FEEDBACK!"; "TAREYTON - DUAL FILTER FOR DOUBLE THE PLEASURE!" The industry later returned to flavor with lines such as "WINSTON TASTES GOOD LIKE A CIGARETTE SHOULD!"

**Principle illustrated:** A new submarket can emerge inside an old category and move through its own sophistication stages.

**Relevance tag:** ad-angles, ad-concepts, headlines-universal
