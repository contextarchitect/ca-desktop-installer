# Rosabella Pattern Summary (Paraphrased Industry Reference)

This file is a structural summary of the 14 Rosabella long-form-static variants. The Rosabella ad library is the canonical industry reference for what long-form-static at production scale looks like. The original copy lives outside this repo as competitive-intel reference; this file captures the structural patterns without verbatim quotes longer than 15 words.

The summary is organized by variant first, then by section pattern. Variant numbers are referenced in `section-structure.md` and `named-patterns.md` as evidence for specific structural rules.

## Variant-by-Variant Notes

**Variant 1.** Hook style: identity-first ("As a [role]"). Section structure: full long-form, ~3,200 words. Authority pattern: Doctor's Surprise. Core feeling: identity. Validates: identification before mechanism (mechanism appears at ~70% of body), the desperation frame (long drive to a specialist), POV stability (first-person sufferer throughout, product introduced organically).

**Variant 2.** Hook style: tension contrast ("I should be [happy state] but [actual state]"). Section structure: full long-form, ~2,900 words. Authority pattern: Classic (named specialist). Core feeling: vindication. Validates: the antecedent story as agitation engine (extensive backstory about a parent's similar condition), three-stage mechanism explanation (named, analogy, differentiated with sourcing specifics).

**Variant 3.** Hook style: family-member POV ("Watching my [family member]"). Section structure: full long-form, ~2,700 words. Authority pattern: Doctor's Skepticism. Core feeling: loss aversion. Validates: the three-lead rule's family-member POV variant, time-delay anchoring (specific weeks and months named throughout).

**Variant 4.** Hook style: location-anchored desperation ("In the parking lot of [specific place]"). Section structure: full long-form, ~3,100 words. Authority pattern: Study/Research. Core feeling: desperation. Validates: the desperation frame canonically (driving to a researcher's office at unusual hours), bridges between sections (every transition has an explicit connector).

**Variant 5.** Hook style: timestamp-anchored ("It was 2 AM"). Section structure: full long-form, ~2,800 words. Authority pattern: Classic. Core feeling: desperation. Validates: the desperation frame's "unusual hours" sub-pattern, the discovery story (specific phrase the protagonist had to look up multiple times before it clicked).

**Variant 6.** Hook style: failed-solution led ("I tried [specific solution] for [duration]"). Section structure: full long-form, ~2,600 words. Authority pattern: Doctor's Surprise. Core feeling: vindication. Validates: failed-solution stack with deep specificity (named medications, named doctors, named durations).

**Variant 7.** Hook style: complaint-framed ("Frustrating thing happened"). Section structure: fake-complaint short-form, ~240 words. Authority pattern: none. Core feeling: implicit social proof. Validates: the fake-complaint short-form structure entirely - customer-to-customer voice, no mechanism, no claims, urgency as close. Used for solution-aware bottom-funnel retargeting.

**Variant 8.** Hook style: physical-symptom anchored. Section structure: full long-form, ~3,400 words. Authority pattern: Classic. Core feeling: betrayal. Validates: the betrayal feeling pattern (industry-failure framing), authority closure (specialist confirms what the protagonist already discovered).

**Variant 9.** Hook style: nurse/professional POV. Section structure: medium long-form, ~1,300 words. Authority pattern: Classic (the protagonist IS the authority). Core feeling: vindication. Validates: the three-lead rule's professional POV variant, medium long-form compression rules (all sections present, each compressed by ~50%).

**Variant 10.** Hook style: contradiction-anchored ("[Common belief] is wrong because"). Section structure: full long-form, ~2,900 words. Authority pattern: Study/Research. Core feeling: betrayal. Validates: the discovery story's "common knowledge inversion" pattern, three-stage mechanism explanation with heavy stage 3 (differentiation with specific numbers).

**Variant 11.** Hook style: anniversary-anchored ("Three years ago this month"). Section structure: full long-form, ~3,000 words. Authority pattern: Doctor's Skepticism. Core feeling: identity. Validates: durability proof section (results sustained at 3 years), mirror moment (avatar-mirroring closer), POV stability across an extended timeframe.

**Variant 12.** Hook style: medical-paperwork anchored ("My lab results said"). Section structure: medium long-form, ~1,200 words. Authority pattern: Doctor's Surprise. Core feeling: vindication. Validates: medium long-form compression with image spec emphasizing medical paperwork as Reddit-native subject, mechanism reveal compressed to stage 1 + stage 2.

**Variant 13.** Hook style: family-member catalyst ("My [family member] said something"). Section structure: full long-form, ~3,100 words. Authority pattern: Classic. Core feeling: loss aversion. Validates: the antecedent story can be triggered by a family-member comment (not only by a personal symptom), the desperation frame with a different unusual decision (not driving, but obsessive late-night research).

**Variant 14.** Hook style: industry-insider POV. Section structure: full long-form, ~2,800 words. Authority pattern: Doctor's Skepticism (insider is the skeptic). Core feeling: betrayal. Validates: professional POV variant, the three-stage mechanism explanation with stage 3 doing heavy industry-comparison work.

## Section-by-Section Abstract Walkthrough

The section structure documented in `section-structure.md` is validated by these variants as follows:

**Hook + headline (in-feed visible).** Variants 1, 5, 11, 13 demonstrate the identity-first or timestamp-first opening at 9 words or fewer. All 14 variants include an identity marker within the first 50 words of the expanded body.

**Identification opener.** Every variant uses first-person voice and includes a defining marker (age, role, condition, family role) within the first paragraph. Variants 1, 9, 14 use professional roles; variants 3, 13 use family-member roles; the remainder use first-degree sufferer roles.

**Antecedent / catalyst story.** Variants 2, 13 demonstrate the parent's-condition antecedent at length (300+ words). Variants 4, 5 demonstrate the personal-event catalyst (a specific scene rather than a long backstory). Both patterns work; the parent's-condition variant is stronger for hereditary conditions.

**POV anchor + decision moment.** Variants 1, 4, 5, 11, 14 demonstrate the doctor-scene refusal canonically. The protagonist refuses the standard solution (medication, surgery, "just monitor it"), and the refusal earns the desperation arc that follows.

**Failed-solution stack.** Variants 6, 9, 10 demonstrate deep-specificity failed-solution stacks (named medications with dosages and durations). Variants 1, 2 use lighter stacks. The deeper stack is stronger for solution-aware audiences who have themselves tried multiple solutions.

**Desperation frame + discovery.** Variants 1, 4, 5, 11, 13, 14 demonstrate the desperation frame canonically. The unusual decisions vary: long-distance drives (variants 1, 4), unusual-hours research (variants 5, 13), travel to a specialist (variant 11), reading academic papers outside one's field (variant 14). The pattern holds across all variants.

**Mechanism reveal.** All 14 variants apply the three-stage explanation. Variants with stronger stage 3 (specific numbers, percentages, sourcing) include 6, 8, 10, 12, 14. Variants with stronger stage 2 (analogy doing the work) include 1, 3, 11.

**Product introduction.** All 14 variants introduce the product from inside the POV. The protagonist names the brand; the brand never names itself. Variant 7 (fake-complaint) is the exception, as it doesn't have a product introduction in the conventional sense.

**Differentiation block.** Variants 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 demonstrate strong differentiation blocks with specific numbers and sourcing claims. The "cold-extracted" pattern (sub-40°C processing preserving high percentages of active compound) is referenced in variants 6 and 14.

**Results timeline.** All 14 variants apply time-delay anchoring before results claims. Common patterns: by day X, by week X, by month X. Variants 11, 12 demonstrate extended-duration timelines (3-year, 8-month durability proof).

**Authority closure.** Variants 1, 2, 6, 8, 11, 12 demonstrate the doctor-confirmation scene (Doctor's Surprise or Classic). Variants 3, 9, 14 demonstrate the doctor-skepticism-then-acceptance arc. Variant 4, 10 demonstrate the study/research validation pattern.

**Durability proof.** Variants 11, 12 demonstrate this section explicitly with multi-year or multi-month timeframes. Most other variants skip it.

**Mirror moment.** Variants 1, 3, 5, 11 demonstrate the avatar-mirroring closer ("If you're [identity marker], you're seeing yourself in this") with strong specificity.

**Risk reversal.** All 14 variants except variant 7 include a risk reversal (90-day money back, no-questions-asked, etc.).

**PS.** All 14 variants include a PS. Variants 1, 5, 11, 13 use callback-to-story PSs (referencing the antecedent character or the decision moment). Variants 6, 9 use future-pacing PSs.

**PPS.** Roughly half of the variants include a PPS. Variants 8, 10, 14 use second-testimonial PPSs; variants 4, 5 use urgency-anchored PPSs.

## Patterns the Library Validates Collectively

Reading the 14 variants together produces these aggregate validations:

- The desperation frame is the canonical discovery vehicle. 11 of 14 variants use it explicitly.
- The three-lead rule produces measurable variant-level performance differences. The Rosabella library has multiple POV variants per angle, and the variants that survive are typically a sufferer + family-member or sufferer + professional pair.
- The fake-complaint short-form is a distinct format used only for solution-aware bottom-funnel. Variant 7 is the only one in this format; it does not blend with the other variants.
- The PS is universally present and high-leverage. The Rosabella library has no variant without a PS.
- POV stability is non-negotiable. No variant breaks first-person voice mid-body. The product is always introduced from inside the POV.
- Time-delay anchoring is universal. No results claim in the library appears without a time anchor.
- The mechanism appears past the 60% mark in every full long-form variant. The mechanism is never front-loaded.

## How to Use This File

When writing a new long-form static, do not reference this file's content as copy material. Reference it as structural validation: "the desperation frame is validated by Rosabella variants 1, 4, 5, 11, 13, 14" is a useful signal that the pattern is robust. Verbatim borrowing is prohibited.

If the writer needs example sentences for a specific section, generate fresh copy from the angle card and the avatar research, then apply the section-structure rules and named patterns. Do not draft from the Rosabella library.
